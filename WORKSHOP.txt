[h1]Traditional Preservation (Yeast)[/h1]

Grow your own yeast from flour and water. Vanilla yeast is loot only, so once the stores are picked clean there's no more bread. This mod lets you ferment a wild culture on a simple wall shelf, the way bakers kept them going for centuries, dry it into yeast cakes, and crumble those into ordinary vanilla Yeast. Hops keep a culture going from one batch to the next.

[b]Build 42 only (42.22 or newer). This is an early release, so back up your save before enabling it. Singleplayer only: multiplayer is not supported.[/b]

Traditional Preservation (Yeast) requires [b]Traditional Preservation (Core)[/b] (TP Core for short): subscribe to it too.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]A buildable Fermenting Shelf that mounts on a wall, with its own art
[*]Each jar on the shelf shows its own progress, from pale to amber to dark brown, in the jar you used: glass or ceramic
[*]Yeast cakes dry on TP Core's Drying Cloth, a sheet held down with two stones
[*]Hop tea feeds a culture: one jar rises into two
[*]Makes real vanilla Yeast, for bread, baking and every mod that uses it
[*]Temperature matters: a warm kitchen in summer, a shelf by the fire in winter
[*]Keeps working while you are away
[/list]

[h2]How it works[/h2]
[olist]
[*][b]Build a Fermenting Shelf[/b] against a wall (Build menu, Furniture): 2 planks, 4 nails and a hammer (kept). Carpentry 2.
[*][b]Make Fermenting Culture[/b] (crafting menu, Cooking): an empty jar (glass or ceramic), 5 uses of flour and half a litre of water from any container. With a fruit it takes 4 days; with potato water (a potato mashed into a pot of water and boiled), 3 and a half.
[*][b]Right-click the shelf[/b] and put up to 5 cultures on it.
[*][b]Refresh Cultures[/b] about once a day: a use of flour and a little water a jar. Unfed for a day a jar stops; for three, it goes off.
[*]After [b]5 in-game days[/b] at a good temperature, take off an Active Culture for each, in its jar. It's alive: about 3 days at 20 C before it's spent.
[*][b]Shape Yeast Cakes[/b]: the culture and 3 uses of cornmeal or flour. The jar comes back.
[*]Lay the cakes on a [b]Drying Cloth[/b] (a Sheet and 2 Stones) where the air moves. About a week later they're dry, and keep.
[*][b]Crumble Yeast Cakes[/b]: one full packet of vanilla Yeast.
[/olist]

One culture gives one full packet of Yeast, which is 10 uses, or about 10 loaves of bread. [b]Steep Hops[/b] (Hops (Dried) in a pot of water, heated) makes Hop Tea, and [b]Feed Culture[/b] turns one culture, an empty jar, hop tea and 5 flour into two, which rise on the shelf in a day and keep twice as long.

[h2]The shelf[/h2]
[list]
[*]Each jar keeps its own clock, so you can stagger them and always have yeast coming. The shelf holds 5.
[*]A culture goes hungry off the shelf too, so keep up the refreshes.
[*]The Fermenting Shelf window lists every jar: its progress, the time left at today's temperature, and when it goes hungry. Hover a row for the temperature and working rate.
[/list]

[h2]Temperature[/h2]
A culture works best from 18 to 30 C. It's at 40% speed at 10 C and 20% at 5 C. At 0 C and below it stops and waits without ever dying ([i]waiting: too cold[/i]). Real heat slows it too.
[list]
[*][b]Open air[/b] (a shelf on an outside wall) and [b]under an open roof[/b]: the weather, hour by hour.
[*][b]Indoors[/b] (a shut room): follows the last 3 days, 2 C warmer in the cold months, up to 2 C warmer in summer (stuffy). 22 C while the power grid is on.
[*][b]Aired room[/b] (a window to the outside open, smashed or empty): the day's average, at most 1 C warmer in winter; 22 C on the grid.
[*][b]In a basement[/b]: follows the ground, a month behind the season: about 5 C from January to March, 13 C from July to September. Three to four weeks a culture in winter, about 9 days in October.
[*][b]By a fire[/b]: any lit fire within 2 tiles holds a shelf in a shut room at 28 C (banked embers 24 C): 5 days in any month. With a window open or outside, the air takes the heat: a fire in an aired room or on a porch gives the air +15 C, a campfire in the open +10 C, up to 28 C. In January that's about 11 days a culture by a porch fire, 20 by a campfire.
[/list]

A kitchen shelf is enough from May to October, taking 5 days a culture in summer. An unheated room in winter sits below freezing, so a culture started there on the first of January waits about three months. In winter, set the shelf by an indoor fire.

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*]Your jar comes back, the same kind you put in.
[*]Yeast cakes die above 35 C, and nothing dries in a shut room: open a window.
[*]Wet cakes keep about 5 days at 20 C. On the cloth they age more slowly as they dry, so fresh cakes where the air moves dry in time in any month; old ones, or ones left in a shut room, go off.
[*]Flour is the real cost: 5 uses per culture, so plan your wheat with yeast in mind.
[*]No vanilla recipe, loot table, hunger or calorie value is changed.
[*]The tiledef number 5127 must be unique among your active mods.
[/list]

[h2]Sandbox options[/h2]
Set the fermenting time at the best temperature (5 days by default), and temperature sensitivity: Off, Forgiving, Normal or Harsh. Off makes every culture take the same time whatever the weather.

[h2]Works well with[/h2]
[b]Traditional Preservation (Vinegar)[/b] uses one packet of Yeast for each vessel run. This mod needs no other mods besides TP Core.
